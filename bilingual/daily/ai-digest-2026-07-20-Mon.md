# AI Builders Digest

## Reader's Briefing / 导读

ChatGPT Work is turning delegation into a product surface. Thibault Sottiaux's examples show the workflow moving from "ask a chatbot" to dictating messy operating tasks, having the agent sort DMs, produce spreadsheets, host sites, manage email, summarize documents, and create business artifacts across plans.
ChatGPT Work 正在把「委派任务」变成产品界面。Thibault Sottiaux 的例子显示，工作流正在从「问 chatbot」变成直接口述混乱的运营任务，让 agent 整理 DM、生成 spreadsheet、托管网站、管理 email、总结文档，并生成业务材料。

Open model competition is becoming harder to dismiss. Guillermo Rauch says internal cyber evals put Kimi K3 near the frontier and Sol ahead at higher cost, while Aaron Levie argues that attempts to gatekeep models will fail because China can now compete near frontier levels.
Open model 竞争越来越难被忽视。Guillermo Rauch 表示，内部 cyber eval 显示 Kimi K3 已接近 frontier，Sol 以更高成本领先；Aaron Levie 则认为，试图 gatekeep model 不会奏效，因为中国已经能在 near-frontier 水平竞争。

Personal evals may matter more than public benchmark worship. Zara Zhang argues everyone should keep a small set of real tasks from their own work and life, while Swyx frames AEO as an actual revenue channel and Europe as an underappreciated AI engineering talent pool.
个人 eval 可能比追逐公开 benchmark 更重要。Zara Zhang 建议每个人都保留一小组来自自己工作和生活的真实任务；Swyx 则把 AEO 视为真实收入渠道，并认为欧洲 AI 工程人才被低估。

Enterprise adoption is still a people-and-context problem. Zara's blunt diagnosis is that AI experts often do not understand the business and business experts often do not understand AI, while builders like Thibault and Peter Yang are showing how adoption happens when agents connect to concrete jobs and playful learning loops.
企业采用 AI 仍然是人和业务上下文的问题。Zara 的诊断很直接：懂 AI 的人常常不懂业务，懂业务的人常常不懂 AI；而 Thibault 和 Peter Yang 这样的 builders 正在展示，当 agent 接到具体工作和轻量学习场景时，采用才会发生。

The AGI debate is being pulled back toward the physical world and human purpose. Jürgen Schmidhuber says true AI cannot be only "behind the screen" because robot hardware still trails human bodies, while Rauch argues "AGI" has aged poorly and "superintelligence" should remain explicitly pro-human.
AGI 讨论正在被重新拉回物理世界和人的目的。Jürgen Schmidhuber 认为 true AI 不能只存在于 "behind the screen"，因为 robot hardware 仍然落后于人体；Rauch 则认为 "AGI" 这个词已经显得过时，"superintelligence" 必须明确服务于人。

## Podcast

Unsupervised Learning featured AI pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber in "Ep 90: AI Pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber on the State of AI Today." The takeaway: Schmidhuber is optimistic about AI technology, skeptical of today's model-company economics, and unusually insistent that true AI must eventually leave the screen and deal with the physical world.
Unsupervised Learning 采访了 AI pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber，标题是 "Ep 90: AI Pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber on the State of AI Today"。核心 takeaway：Schmidhuber 对 AI 技术乐观，对当下 model company 的经济性谨慎，而且特别强调 true AI 最终必须离开屏幕、处理物理世界。
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RKjR8DQ40po

Schmidhuber says that, from a cosmic perspective, the goal of building AI smarter than himself is as close as it felt in the 1970s, but the timeline may still be years or decades because "true AI is not just the AI behind the screen." Today's systems pass many language tests, yet robot hardware remains inferior to human bodies; his sharpest framing is that "you can't have AGI just behind the screen."
Schmidhuber 说，从宇宙尺度看，造出比自己更聪明的 AI 这个目标，和他 1970 年代第一次提出愿望时一样近；但具体是几年还是几十年并不确定，因为 "true AI is not just the AI behind the screen"。今天的系统可以通过很多语言测试，但 robot hardware 仍然不如人体；他最尖锐的说法是，"you can't have AGI just behind the screen"。

That physical constraint makes his view more grounded than the standard software-only acceleration story. Recursive self-improvement is real in scaled-back forms, from self-modifying neural networks to older Godel-machine ideas, but he does not treat it as an automatic moat for current model companies. The more contrarian thread is financial: he is very optimistic about AI as a technology while expecting today's capex boom and model-company valuations to face a painful correction.
这个物理约束让他的观点比纯软件加速叙事更接地气。Recursive self-improvement 的确存在于一些缩小版形式里，从 self-modifying neural networks 到更早的 Godel-machine 思路，但他并不把它视为当前 model companies 的自动 moat。更逆向的主线是金融判断：他非常看好 AI 作为技术，但预计今天的 capex boom 和 model-company valuations 会经历痛苦修正。
