- AI & I by Every: "Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social (Best of the Pod)"

  **The Takeaway:** The next breakout AI consumer product will be social: a multiplayer, network-effect experience where expert users make the technology easy for everyone else.

  Benchmark partner Sarah, who was early at Pinterest and studied philosophy, reads consumer tech history as a slider that moves from deeply technical founding teams to product geniuses as infrastructure matures. Google was roughly 95% back-end engineering; by the time of Pinterest, Snap, and Instagram, the CEOs were product geniuses. AI, she argues, is still early on that curve. ChatGPT is "just a text box," in the way Google was, and custom GPTs frustrate her: "It's clearly made by a team that is unbelievably capable but isn't social." Her bet is that someone will build a UGC community where expert prompt users are findable, followable, and trusted, making the technology dramatically easier for everyone else, and that this place could eventually cannibalize ChatGPT. She also separates the "worky" bucket, where ChatGPT and Claude live, from the "personal" bucket of AI friends, and argues social products need status-seeking mechanics, in the spirit of Eugene Wei. On network effects, she distinguishes flywheels that are just "words on a slide" from early evidence, citing Agentio, a marketplace where LLMs automated the friction that long held the creator economy back. And she expects AI to reshape VC decision-making through personal training data, pre-mortems, and talent scores, while humans stay the decider: "You're not asking the LLM to give you the answer... you're asking it to probe your thinking."
