模型竞争越来越围绕困难任务表现和实际可用性展开。OpenAI 的 Thibault Sottiaux 表示，GPT-6.2 Astra 在处理困难任务时很强，并提到团队在 AMD 与 Jalapeño 芯片优化上的结果，同时承认外界对其沟通风格的反馈。信号不只是原始能力，还包括能否长期融入工作流；Sottiaux 表示沟通风格问题正在改进。

Agent 正从一次性回答走向协同系统。Box CEO Aaron Levie 描述了一种新阶段：agent 可以并行展开、处理更大的上下文、长时间追逐目标，然后设计、测试、部署并持续优化自己的工作。Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 推出 Vercel board，把它定义为不绑定模型与 harness 的 agent 消息板，是软件工厂、自我优化系统和科学研究的基础设施。Replit CEO Amjad Masad 则提到 Replit Missions，可以让用户看到 agent 如何协作和对话。

自建还是购买的问题又变得更尖锐。曾在 Anthropic Claude Code 工作的 Thariq 认为，大多数人不会真的自己生成软件，因为这需要先知道自己想要什么，而且好的软件是一组鲜明的观点。Notion CEO 兼创始人 Ivan Zhao 也划出类似界线：一类工具应当让用户自由修改，另一类产品值得由专家精心打磨。他用 Lego 积木与 clay 作比喻。

Notion's strategic answer is context, not interface code. Zhao says agents can generate procedural logic and UI, while business data, semantics and know-how are more durable and sit outside any single model. Notion 的战略答案是上下文，而不是界面代码。Zhao 认为，agent 可以生成流程逻辑和 UI，而企业数据、语义和 know-how 更持久，也位于任何单一模型之外。按他的说法，token 的反面是 content；Notion 希望成为这些上下文的保管者，在保留人类协作和权限护栏的同时，向不同 agent 开放。

资本与分发正在奖励复利，而不是一次性胜利。Rauch 表示，Vercel AI Gateway 的 token 需求自年初以来大约每月复合增长 40%，他预计行业会先扩张、后整合。Nikunj Kothari 建议创始人在第一页就讲清楚雄心实现后的二阶和三阶回报；Aditya Agarwal 认为，专注 AI 的早期投资人需要更强的风险偏好和非共识的前沿押注；Zara Zhang 则认为，每周积累一点小范围传播，胜过一次巨大的爆红。

### Notion’s Founder on the One Thing Your AI Is Missing
**AI & I by Every**

**The Takeaway:** The durable layer of an AI-native workplace is not interface code but the context, data and know-how that let humans and agents do better work together.

**核心结论：** AI 原生工作环境中最持久的层，不是界面代码，而是让人类与 agent 更好协作的上下文、数据和 know-how。

Ivan Zhao, CEO and founder of Notion, sees coding agents as a late arrival of an old promise: computing as a medium everyone can read and write, not a priesthood of application builders. Notion began with that ambition, using blocks and databases to give non-programmers software-like structure. Zhao still loves the spirit of vibe coding, but he separates the freedom to modify your tools from the question of what should be custom-built. A calendar or an enterprise system often benefits from expert craft, maintenance and security. His metaphor is Lego versus clay: raw code gives people clay, while well-designed primitives give them reusable structure.

Ivan Zhao，Notion 的 CEO 兼创始人，把 coding agent 看作一个旧承诺的迟到兑现：计算应当成为每个人都能读写的媒介，而不是应用开发者的小圈子。Notion 从这一雄心出发，用 block 和 database 让非程序员也能获得类似软件的结构。Zhao 依然喜欢 vibe coding 的精神，但他把“可以修改工具的自由”和“什么应该被定制开发”分开。日历或企业系统往往需要专业打磨、维护和安全保障。他的比喻是 Lego 与 clay：原始代码给人一团黏土，设计良好的原语则提供可复用的结构。

The deeper strategic bet is context. Agents can generate procedural logic and UI, and models change quickly, so Notion is positioning itself as the keeper of a business's data, semantics and know-how. "What is opposite of tokens? In some sense, it's content. It's compressed tokens. It's your know-how, your knowledge." Zhao wants that context to flow to different agents under permissions, while a team of humans and a team of agents share a visible working canvas. Notion's near-term goal is an out-of-the-box context and knowledge solution for a 50-person company.

更深层的战略押注是上下文。Agent 可以生成流程代码和 UI，模型也会快速变化，因此 Notion 把自己定位为企业数据、语义和 know-how 的保管者。"What is opposite of tokens? In some sense, it's content. It's compressed tokens. It's your know-how, your knowledge." Zhao 希望这些上下文能在权限约束下流向不同的 agent，同时让一组人类与一组 agent 共享可见的工作画布。Notion 的近期目标，是为 50 人公司提供开箱即用的上下文与知识解决方案。

Zhao also warns that capability without understanding is a trap. Chat can become TikTok-like, producing motion without durable comprehension. His answer is better abstractions and visualization, including using a Kanban board to orchestrate agents. "I don't think we should surrender our understanding. Definitely not without putting up a fight."

Zhao 也提醒，有能力却没有理解是一种陷阱。聊天可能变得像 TikTok，产生动作却没有持久理解。他的答案是更好的抽象和可视化，包括用 Kanban board 编排 agent。"I don't think we should surrender our understanding. Definitely not without putting up a fight."
