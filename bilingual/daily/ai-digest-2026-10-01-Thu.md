### Aaron Levie: CEO of Box

Aaron Levie says there is a huge opportunity right now to be the deployment layer for AI into the economy, because changing out enterprise workflows takes far more work than anyone realizes or would prefer. He lists what that involves: moving legacy systems to the cloud, updating data organization and access, connecting software to agents in new ways, reengineering workflows for agents, figuring out human-in-the-loop, generating and maintaining evals, and continually updating the system as new models and capabilities arrive. His key distinction is that AI is not the same as deploying software: traditional software implementation covered a well-understood category of technology, after which the customer ran it themselves, while AI agents deliver actual work augmentation inside a process, a completely different implementation and enablement problem.
